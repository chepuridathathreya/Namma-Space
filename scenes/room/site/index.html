How to move

      
This is a 3D scan of a real room, built from one phone video. Walls and furniture stop you.

    

    

      
WalkKeyboard: W A S D or the arrow keys. Touch: use the joystick.

      
LookMouse: click the scene, then move the mouse. Esc releases it. Touch: drag the scene.

      
Find a placePress / or choose Find a place. Pick a result, then Walk there or Fly there.

      
Other keysShift runs, Space jumps, R returns to the door, M shows the walkable map, N the live map.
