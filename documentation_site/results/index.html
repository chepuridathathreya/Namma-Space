PSNR and SSIM: higher is better. LPIPS: lower is better. Scores are measured on frames held out from training. The walkable floor is the area your body centre can reach after the 25 cm margin, so it is smaller than the room. The Early Bedroom predates our evaluation step, so no held-out figures exist for it.

Training used one NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell GPU. Peak memory was about 16 GB for the Room, measured over the whole card. Within the hallway family, a first run scored 23.56 dB with 709 frames and the improved run 26.46 dB with 868 frames. The Room is a different scene, so that comparison shows pipeline progress rather than a like-for-like gain.

Frame rate in the browser

On a laptop with both an integrated and a discrete GPU the browser may run on the integrated one. With all splats drawn we measured 24 to 38 fps on the integrated GPU and 170 to 180 fps on the RTX 3060 laptop GPU. A web page cannot choose the GPU, so the viewer measures its frame rate about 2.5 seconds after loading and, below 35 fps, steps down to fewer splats and a lower render resolution and says so.
